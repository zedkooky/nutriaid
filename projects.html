Our portfolio

Our projects

Current programmes and past work in agribusiness, climate adaptation, nutrition and financial inclusion across Zambia.

Active

Improved Socio-Economic Status of Women, Youth and Marginalised Groups

A triple-nexus programme of economic development, gender justice and peacebuilding, delivered through Farmer Field Schools with irrigation, small livestock, cooking demonstrations and Savings for Change.

  • Where Gwembe, Namwala, Kaoma and Mongu
  • Funder Irish Aid, through Oxfam Zambia, with Women for Change and YWCA
  • Scale 15,600 participants, 50 Farmer Field Schools, 4 Centres of Excellence
  • Period 2023 to 2028
Climate adaptation
Active

Community-Based Adaptation (CBA-SCALE Southern Africa+)

Building community capacity to manage climate-related risks to livelihoods and food security.

  • Where 12 wards of Zimba district, Southern Province
  • Funder CARE, with EU support
  • Period 2023 to 2028
Food processing
Active

Food Loss Reduction Solution

Growing whole-grain consumption through institutional markets: linking whole-grain processors and the smallholders who supply them.

  • Where Choma, Mumbwa, Kapiri-Mposhi, Chipata and Lundazi
  • Funder AGRA
  • Scale 26 SME processors, 38,200 smallholder farmers
  • Period 2026 to 2029
Seed systems
Active

Strengthening Legume Seed Systems (Z4ABC)

Establishing Farmer Field Schools where women and youth produce legume seed (soya beans, pigeon peas and velvet beans) for an anchor seed company.

  • Where Nyimba, Petauke and Katete
  • Funder European Union, with CIFOR-ICRAF
  • Scale 12 Farmer Field Schools, 500 women and youth
  • Period 2026 to 2027
Organisational strength
Active

Civil Society Strengthening (Shift the Power Zambia)

Strengthening locally led civil society: the grant helps NAT address capacity barriers and secure its long-term sustainability.

  • Funder Zambia Governance Foundation, with Tearfund
  • Period 2026 to 2030
Climate adaptation
Completed

Strengthening Climate Resilience in the Kafue Sub-Basin (SCRiKA)

Climate-smart farming and resilience infrastructure, including boreholes and goat, pig and poultry housing, for communities in the Kafue basin.

  • Where Itezhi-Tezhi, Mumbwa, Chibombo, Kafue and other districts
  • Funder African Development Bank, through the Government of Zambia
Nutrition
Completed

Empowering Communities to Scale Up Good Nutrition (EC2SIGN)

Joining agriculture, health and WASH to improve nutrition for pregnant and lactating women and young children, with cooking demonstrations and solar drying.

  • Where Mpongwe, Masaiti and Lufwanyama, Copperbelt
  • Funder European Union, with Save the Children
Irrigation
Completed

Wealth Creation through Irrigation in North-Western Province (WIN)

Irrigation training and collective marketing for horticultural farmers supplying the mining sector.

  • Where North-Western Province
  • Funder European Union, with International Development Enterprises
Markets

Strengthening Agricultural Inputs and Output Markets (SAIOMA)

Strengthening farmer organisations, agro-dealers and community aggregators, and linking producers to commodity buyers.

  • Where Eastern and Central provinces
  • Funder USAID and AGRA
