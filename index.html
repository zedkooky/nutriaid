Who we are

Established in 2007, driving rural development.

Nutri-Aid Trust is a leading Zambian agribusiness NGO dedicated to transforming the agricultural landscape. We focus on bridging the gap between smallholder farmers and commercial markets, fostering enterprise development that ensures food security and economic stability.

Our institutional approach prioritizes scalable, long-term solutions over short-term aid.

Our expertise

Strategic enterprise solutions

01

Agro-dealer Development

We build robust supply chains by equipping local agro-dealers with business management skills, inventory systems, and credit access, ensuring farmers have reliable inputs when they need them.

02

Technical Training

Nutri-Aid Trust ensures high-yield agronomic practices and climate-smart agriculture education delivered through extensive field days and localized demonstration plots.

03

Market Linkages

Connecting smallholder cooperatives directly to commercial buyers and processors to guarantee fair pricing and secure off-take agreements.

04

Financial Inclusion

Facilitating access to micro-credit, savings groups, and digital financial services tailored for the unique cash-flow cycles of rural agriculture.

05

Data Analytics

Leveraging mobile technology to track yields, monitor input usage, and provide actionable intelligence to regional stakeholders.

How it connects

From the field to the market, one chain.

Inputs, knowledge, finance and buyers, joined into a single system so smallholders aren't left to bridge the gaps alone.
